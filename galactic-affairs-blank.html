Design by hand: add text boxes and images, drag them by their top bar, and resize from the bottom-right corner. Use the A− / A+ control on a box to change its text size, or type an exact number. Pink guides snap to the edges and centers of other elements and the canvas; hold Alt to move freely. Drag the grip below the page to make the canvas taller when a story needs more room. Use − / + to scale the view for a sharper screenshot. Add an image with the button or by dragging a file onto the canvas. When finished, click Clean view; the toolbar and handles all disappear so they can't end up in your shot. Then snip around the bulletin (Win + Shift + S), or click Export PNG to save the page as an image at a fixed 1200 px width, so every issue comes out the same size. Export text copies every box in reading order, ready to paste onto the Trello card. Download draft saves all your pages to a file you can send to another Tribune member, and Load draft opens one. The Edit button, top-left, brings everything back.
